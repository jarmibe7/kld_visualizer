What you are seeing

The target distribution P is a fixed mixture of Gaussians. The blue curve is P; the orange line is the learned Gaussian Q. Reverse KL tends to place mass on a single mode and can ignore low-probability regions, whereas forward KL spreads mass to cover the target support.

Reverse KL $D_{KL}(Q\|P)=\sum_x Q(x)\log\frac{Q(x)}{P(x)}$

Forward KL $D_{KL}(P\|Q)=\sum_x P(x)\log\frac{P(x)}{Q(x)}$
